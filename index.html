01 / The problem

What is a traffic jam?

A traffic jam occurs when the number of vehicles using a road exceeds the road's capacity, causing vehicles to slow down or stop. It can affect commuters, businesses, emergency services, and the environment.

02 / Common causes

Why does congestion happen?

🚗

Too many vehicles

When many people travel at the same time, roads can become overloaded.

🚧

Road work

Construction, repairs, and blocked lanes reduce the space available for traffic.

🚦

Signal delays

Poorly coordinated traffic signals can create long queues at intersections.

🅿️

Illegal parking

Vehicles parked in busy lanes can narrow roads and slow everyone down.

🚌

Unplanned stopping

Stopping in the middle of a road to pick up or drop off passengers disrupts flow.

🌧️

Bad weather

Heavy rain, poor visibility, or flooding can make journeys slower and less predictable.

03 / Practical solutions

Small changes can move us forward.

01

Improve public transport
Reliable buses and trains can reduce the number of private vehicles on the road.

02

Follow traffic rules
Respect signals, lane markings, pedestrian crossings, and parking restrictions.

03

Travel at different times
Flexible work and school schedules can help reduce peak-hour pressure.

04

Walk, cycle, or share rides
Choose a suitable lower-impact travel option whenever possible.
